# Dept 2: 教研情报部 (`Admissions Fact & Policy Intelligence`)

## Mandatory Verification Checklist
1. **Live 2026 QS World University Rankings**: Never rely on memory. Verify exact `2026 QS` rank for every university mentioned.
2. **Official 2026 / 26Fall Tuition & Living Costs**: Verify non-local tuition in original currency (`GBP`, `HKD`, `MOP`, `AUD`, `SGD`, `MYR`, `NZD`, `USD`) and convert at current 2026 exchange rates (`GBP ~9.2+`, `HKD ~0.92`, `MOP ~0.89`, `AUD ~4.7+`, `USD ~7.1+`). Include realistic off-campus rent vs on-campus dorm availability.
3. **Portal, Language & Pathway Boundaries**:
   - Exact CET-6 acceptance vs mandatory IELTS/TOEFL at application time;
   - Exact International Year One (IY1 / Diploma, 3-yr total) vs Foundation (`1+3 = 4-yr total`) lists;
   - Exact Gaokao direct-entry percentages & single-subject English/Math veto lines.
4. **Buyer-Side Disqualification (`劝退画像与隐形代价`)**: Document graduation failure risks, block-teaching intensity, intake delay traps, or mainland HR recognition caveats for every option.
